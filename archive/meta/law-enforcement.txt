Our mission is to build the future of human connection and the technology that makes it possible. But none of this is possible if people don’t feel safe on our technologies. Our approach to safety includes writing clear policies about what is and isn’t allowed on our platforms, developing sophisticated technology to detect and prevent abuse from happening in the first place, and providing helpful tools and resources for people to control their experience or get help.
