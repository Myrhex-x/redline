# ScanRecords editorial policy

ScanRecords publishes **observations, not conclusions**. A recorded change
means a document changed — nothing more. These rules exist so the archive
stays worth citing.

## Corrections

Factual errors are corrected as soon as they are identified. Corrections are
visible: the fix is a public commit, and materially wrong published claims get
a correction note rather than a silent edit. The git history of this
repository is the correction log.

## Vendor responses

Before ScanRecords publishes any interpretive claim about a named company
(anything beyond the raw record of "this document changed on this date"), that
company is offered a response window of **14 days**. Responses are published
verbatim alongside the claim. The raw archive itself is automated and factual;
it is not subject to prior review by anyone, including us.

## Takedown requests

The archive preserves unmodified copies of legal documents that their owners
published to the general public, as a public-interest record. Takedown
requests are answered, and both the request and the answer are published,
unless the requester demonstrates a legal obligation to the contrary.
